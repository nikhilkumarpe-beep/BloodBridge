Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about blood requests

How quickly can I get blood after making a request?

Response time depends on blood type availability and urgency. For critical cases, we prioritize the request and aim to find donors within hours. Non-emergency requests may take 24-48 hours to process.

Is there a cost for requesting blood?

LifeBlood does not charge for connecting patients with donors. However, there may be standard processing and testing fees charged by the blood bank or hospital for the blood units.

What information do I need to provide when making a request?

You'll need to provide the patient's full name, blood type, hospital details, contact information, and the date when blood is needed. Additional medical information may be required for verification.

Can I request blood for someone else?

Yes, you can request blood on behalf of someone else. Please ensure you have the patient's consent and accurate medical information before submitting the request.

What happens after I submit a blood request?

After submission, our team will verify the request with the hospital. Once verified, we'll start searching for matching donors. You'll receive updates via phone or email about the status of your request.
