Why Donate Blood?

Blood donation is a simple act that has a huge impact.

Save Lives

Every drop counts. Your donation can help accident victims, surgery patients, and Thalassemia patients.

Quick & Easy

The entire process takes less than an hour. We organize camps across major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore.

Health Check

Get a free mini-physical. We check your pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin levels.

Did You Know?

Interesting facts about blood donation that might surprise you.

1 in 3 Lives

One pint of blood can save up to 3 lives. Your single donation makes a massive difference.

Every 2 Seconds

Someone in India needs blood every two seconds. The demand is constant and urgent.

Cannot be Made

Blood cannot be manufactured in a lab. It can only come from generous donors like you.

Universal Donor

Type O-Negative blood is the universal donor type and is critical in emergency situations.
