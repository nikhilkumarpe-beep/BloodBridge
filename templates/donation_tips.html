Blood Donation Tips

Everything you need to know for a safe and successful donation.

Before You Donate

Hydrate Well

Drink plenty of water or fruit juice the night before and morning of your donation.

Eat Iron-Rich Foods

Eat foods like spinach, red meat, fish, poultry, beans, and iron-fortified cereals.

Get Rest

Ensure you get a good night's sleep before your donation day.

Avoid Alcohol

Avoid drinking alcohol for 24 hours prior to donation.

During Donation

  • Wear clothing with sleeves that can be raised above the elbow.
  • Relax! The actual donation only takes about 10 minutes.
  • Let the staff know if you have a preferred arm or vein.
  • Listen to music or chat with the staff to stay relaxed.

After You Donate

Rest & Snack

Rest for a few minutes and enjoy the refreshments provided.

Drink Fluids

Drink extra liquids (4 cups) over the next 24 hours.

Avoid Strenuous Activity

Avoid heavy lifting or vigorous exercise for the rest of the day.

Keep Bandage On

Keep the strip bandage on for the next several hours.
